Calculation engine (macro-enabled workbook)
⚙️
Per-unit aggregation
& KPI derivation
  • Filter rows by company / org unit (Sheet 4)
  • Sum raw material quantities (Sheet 1)
  • Sum energy & water quantities (Sheet 2)
  • Sum waste quantities by type (Sheet 3)
  • Multiply quantities × CO₂ factors (Sheet 5)
  • Derive product output = material − waste + pkg
  • Calculate specific KPIs (per kg product)
  • Rank top materials / top wastes by CO₂ share
  • Compute year-on-year % change (2024 → 2025)
